Concrete in the splash zone doesn't fail quietly.

Chlorides get in through the surface, reach the reinforcement and start it corroding. Rust takes up more room than steel, the cover cracks and spalls, and the section that was designed to carry the load gets smaller every year. On prestressed piles the tendons are the structure, and once they are corroding the margin goes quickly.

By the time it is visible from the deck, the damage is usually well ahead of what you can see. The usual answers are to patch what shows, to post weight restrictions, or to replace the structure. We offer a fourth: establish what each element can still carry, repair the ones that need it to a certified design, and protect them for the rest of their life.

How we do it

Survey everything, repair what needs it, and record what was done. Each step is ours to deliver.

Survey and rank

Every pile is inspected: visual condition, ultrasonic pulse velocity to find voids and delamination, and half-cell potential readings to find active corrosion in the reinforcement or tendons. Access by rope access technicians, from the water or by drone-carried sensors where the structure allows. Each element gets a condition score, so repairs go where they are needed and sound elements are left alone.

Engineer

Residual capacity of each damaged element against the loads it actually carries. A repair design to a certified basis: jacket type, grout specification, surface preparation and the sealing details that keep the chlorides out afterwards.

Encapsulate

Loose and spalled concrete is removed and the substrate prepared. A jacket is fitted around the damaged length and the annulus filled with structural grout, which bonds to the pile and restores the section. The jacket stays in place as a permanent barrier, sealed top and bottom.

Install in service

Rig-less, no hot work, from MADCON's own dive and access spread. The jetty or trestle keeps operating and the programme is sequenced around your berthing or traffic. Productivity is planned per pile, so a large programme has a schedule you can hold us to.

Record

As-built records for every element go into your integrity system, or into ours, so the next inspection starts from what was done, not from a search for paperwork.

What encapsulation does

A patch repair treats the symptom. Encapsulation restores the structural section and stops the mechanism that caused the damage.

  • Section restored by structural grout bonded to prepared concrete
  • Reinforcement and tendons re-passivated and protected
  • Permanent jacket keeps seawater and chlorides out
  • No demolition, no hot work, no shutdown
  • Decades of protection with a known inspection regime

Questions we are usually asked

What is pile encapsulation?

A jacket is fitted around the damaged length of a concrete pile and the gap between jacket and pile is filled with structural grout. The grout bonds to the prepared concrete, restores the lost section and protects the reinforcement; the sealed jacket keeps seawater and chlorides out for the rest of the structure's life.

Does the jetty or trestle have to close while you work?

No. The method is rig-less, needs no hot work and is installed from the water or by rope access while the structure stays in service. Work is sequenced around your operations.

How do you decide which piles to repair?

We survey every pile first, using visual inspection, ultrasonic pulse velocity to find voids, and half-cell potential readings to find active reinforcement corrosion. Each pile is then ranked by condition, so the worst are repaired first and sound piles are left alone.

What structures do you repair?

Jetties, piers, trestles, dolphins, bridge piers, cooling-water intakes and outfalls, sea walls, offshore concrete structures and any reinforced or prestressed concrete in a marine or splash-zone environment.

Where do you work?

From Newcastle upon Tyne across the UK, and worldwide with iSIMS LLC in Houston and iSIMS Trinidad Limited. MADCON's crews have repaired more than 2,500 concrete pilings on a single causeway in the United States and have worked on marine structures for 38 years.

What does EPCI mean for a concrete repair?

One contract covers engineering, procurement, construction and installation. We hold the design authority throughout, MADCON delivers the materials and the offshore work, and the interfaces between survey, design and installation sit with us rather than with you.
