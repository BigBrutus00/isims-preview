No two assets are the same. Neither is our plan for them.

We don't sell a menu of services. We start with your structure: its age, its condition, the life left in the field, and what your regulator and insurer need to see. Then we build the plan that gets the most out of it, drawing on our engineers, our software and, where it's needed, our own repair crews.

The result is a programme sized to the asset, not to the industry average: inspection where risk actually sits, repair only where it pays back, and the evidence to show both.

What that looks like in practice

The asset

A marine trestle on hundreds of concrete piles, with spalling and exposed reinforcement in the splash zone.

Our plan

Every pile surveyed and ranked by condition, so the worst are done first. Each repaired pile is encapsulated: a jacket fitted round the damaged length and filled with structural grout, restoring the section and sealing the steel from seawater. Designed by us, installed by MADCON's crews with the trestle in use.

What you get

Piles back to design capacity with decades of protection, no demolition, no shutdown, and a programme with a productivity rate you can plan around.

Measured, not assumed.

We fit sensors to operating platforms and record how they really move. The measured natural periods calibrate our analysis models and remove conservatism that would otherwise make a healthy structure look like it is failing.

The result is a truer picture of remaining capacity, and fewer repairs that were never needed.

iJacket™, the new fixed foundation.

Our patented iJacket™ carries the same deck, wells or wind turbine as a conventional X-braced jacket and meets the same storm, hurricane and earthquake demands. It does it with far less steel and a limited number of nodes.

Fewer nodes means less cutting, fitting, welding and NDT in the yard, repeatable details that suit series production, and fewer fatigue-sensitive joints to inspect for the rest of its life. Lighter jackets need a smaller installation spread, and many can be lifted rather than launched.
